## Shanghai — Quick Practical Guide

## Getting Around

- **From Pudong Airport to City**: Maglev → metro connection = ~30 min total. Or take Metro Line 2 directly (slower but cheaper)
- **Hongqiao Airport**: Directly connected by Metro Line 2 and 10
- **Metro**: The fastest way around Shanghai. Trains run late into the evening, but last-train times differ by line and station — check the current schedule from the operator before relying on a late journey
- **Bund / Pudong**: Cross the Huangpu River by ferry at the Bund — great views. Per the Shanghai municipal government, pedestrian ferry fares are ¥2

## Best Itineraries

### Day 1 — Classic Shanghai
1. Morning: People's Square → Nanjing Road → Bund
2. Lunch: Local Shanghai restaurant
3. Afternoon: Pudong skyline walk (Lujiazui) + Oriental Pearl Tower
4. Evening: Night cruise on Huangpu River (optional, ~$25)

### Day 2 — Arts & Culture
1. Morning: Yuyuan Garden + Old Town
2. Lunch: Soup dumplings at Jia Jia Tang Bao
3. Afternoon: Former French Settlement (walk the tree-lined streets) + Tianzifang
4. Evening: Live house or jazz bar

| Dish | What | Where |

## Shanghai-Specific Tips

- **Tipping is not expected** at restaurants, but rounding up is fine
- **French Concession** (Former French Settlement) area is very walkable and photogenic
- **English is more widely spoken** in Shanghai than most other Chinese cities
- **Taxi tip**: Tell the driver your destination's general area, not just the exact address — e.g., "Pudong, Lujiazui"
- **Typhoon season** runs July-September — check weather before day trips
- **Shopping**: West Nanjing Road = luxury malls; Tianzifang = artsy boutiques; Qipu Road = cheap clothes

## 10 Essential Chinese Phrases

Save these in your phone's notes — they're your survival kit:

| English | Chinese | Pinyin |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Hello | 你好 | Nǐ hǎo |
| Thank you | 谢谢 | Xièxie |
| Where is...? | ...在哪里 | ...zài nǎlǐ |
| How much? | 多少钱 | Duōshao qián |
| Too expensive | 太贵了 | Tài guì le |
| Please speak English | 请说英语 | Qǐng shuō Yīngyǔ |
| I don't understand | 我不懂 | Wǒ bù dǒng |
| Help! | 救命 | Jiùmìng |
| Restroom | 卫生间 | Wèishēngjiān |
| Cash | 现金 | Xiànjīn |

## Translation Apps Ranked for China

1. **WeChat Translation** — built-in, works without Google
2. **Google Translate** — best quality, but needs VPN
3. **Baidu Translate** — works natively, but English translation quality is lower

Google Translate can also offer voice translation and offline language packs depending on the language and current app support.

## Pro Tip: Show, Don't Tell

- Show your passport when confused — people will immediately realize you're foreign
- Show photos of what you want (restaurant dishes, landmarks)
- Point at things and say "zhège" (this one)

# Internet in China — The Practical Guide

Internet access in China has two parts: **getting connected** and **working around the Great Firewall**. Here's what you need to know:

## What's Blocked

Without a VPN, these typically don't work:
- Google, Gmail, Google Maps
- Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Snapchat, TikTok (Douyin is the Chinese version)
- YouTube, Twitter/X, Reddit
- Slack, Discord, Zoom (sometimes)
- Many western news sites

## What Works Natively

These work without VPN:
- WeChat (messaging)
- Alipay / WeChat Pay
- Baidu (Chinese Google)
- Gaode Maps / Baidu Maps (navigation)
- Didi (ride-hailing)
- Ele.me / Meituan (food delivery)
- Xiaohongshu (RedNote — Instagram-like but for China)

## Top Tip

**Buy your eSIM and install your VPN before leaving home.** Once you land in China, downloading VPN apps or ordering eSIMs from blocked sites becomes much harder.

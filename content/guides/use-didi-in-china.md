## Using DiDi — China's Uber

DiDi is China's dominant ride-hailing app. It works much like Uber or Grab. The important thing is getting the **right version** of the app and having your addresses ready.

## Address Tips

- **Hotel addresses** in English often don't work perfectly. Copy/paste the Chinese version from your hotel's booking confirmation
- **Landmarks** work well — "Tiananmen Square" or "Shanghai Tower"
- **Gaode Maps** (高德地图) will show you the Chinese name of any location — use that

## Safety Notes

- Share your trip with a friend via DiDi's "Share Trip" feature — it sends live location
- Drivers see only your first name (or pseudonym)
- Car license plate numbers are shown before pickup — check they match
- At night, be extra careful. Consider taking a taxi from a hotel entrance where staff can see you

## Chinese Phrases for Taxi Drivers

Even with DiDi, these are useful:
- "我在这里" (wǒ zài zhèlǐ) = "I'm here"
- "往前走" (wǎng qián zǒu) = "Go straight ahead"
- "在右边" (zài yòu biān) = "On the right side"
- "谢谢" (xièxie) = "Thank you"
